# SoundForge
FPGA Audio Processor Carrier Board:
This is a project I started the summer after my sophomore year of college. It combines a mix of analog electronic design, digital design and software engineering into one cool project. I love music and I was thinking of a cool project to work on that combines what I am learning in college with a passion of mine. This was the byproduct. Still in development!
